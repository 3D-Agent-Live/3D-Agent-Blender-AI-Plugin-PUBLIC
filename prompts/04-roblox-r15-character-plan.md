# Game character with a dimensioned plan

Source: [3d-agent.com/docs/example-prompts](https://3d-agent.com/docs/example-prompts) (copied 2026-09-25).

Turn on [Plan mode](https://3d-agent.com/docs/chat#plan-mode), attach one clear front view of the character, and send:

> Build this character as a Roblox R15 avatar, 6 studs tall. Plan it first: list every part with its size and position, the rig, one 512 × 512 color texture, a budget of 5,000 to 8,000 triangles, and checks for symmetry, feet at ground level and a closed mesh.

Review the plan's measurements, then click **Implement**. The plan turns a picture into numbers the agent can build from. This one took about 2 prompts.
