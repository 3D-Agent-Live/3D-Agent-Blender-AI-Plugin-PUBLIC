# Low-poly game building with strict rules

Source: [3d-agent.com/docs/example-prompts](https://3d-agent.com/docs/example-prompts) (copied 2026-09-25).

> Build a low-poly cafe, 20 × 15 units. Kitchen at the back, order desk at the front, walkways at least 2 units wide. Use only boxes and cylinders, flat colors, no UVs. Keep it under 10,000 triangles, with manifold meshes and applied transforms.

With the technical limits up front, the model is ready for the engine without cleanup.
