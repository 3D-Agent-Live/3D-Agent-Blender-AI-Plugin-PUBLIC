# Floor plan in three checked steps

Source: [3d-agent.com/docs/example-prompts](https://3d-agent.com/docs/example-prompts) (copied 2026-09-25).

1. *Create a floor slab 4.6 × 4.8 × 0.1 m, four exterior walls 2.7 m high and an interior wall at x = 2.2 m. Check: no gaps at the corners.*
2. *Cut two doors, 0.8 × 2.1 m, in the interior wall and the front wall. Check: no boolean artifacts.*
3. *Add three windows with a sill height of 0.9 m on the south wall. Check: no floating geometry.*

Each step gives exact numbers and says how to check the result.
