# Prop from one precise sentence

Source: [3d-agent.com/docs/example-prompts](https://3d-agent.com/docs/example-prompts) (copied 2026-09-25).

> A desk lamp with an articulated two-joint arm and a round metal shade, in a bent working pose.

It names the parts, the material and the pose. Pro and Ultra can use subagents on prompts like this.
