# Seasonal variation of a scene

Source: [3d-agent.com/docs/example-prompts](https://3d-agent.com/docs/example-prompts) (copied 2026-09-25).

1. *Create a small Minecraft-style floating island with a house, trees and a waterfall.*
2. *Make it winter.*

Follow-ups build on the existing scene, so a short prompt is enough for a big change.
